Hi, I'm Justin.

I am an undergraduate mechanical engineering student at The Cooper Union for the Advancement of Science and Art, expecting to graduate in May 2028.

My engineering focus bridges analytical modeling with physical execution. I enjoy turning theoretical analysis into functional, high-reliability mechanical systems—whether that means deriving dynamic transfer functions in MATLAB, detailing CAD assemblies in Creo Parametric, or standing at a manual mill cutting aluminum to ±0.002" tolerances.

Outside of the machine shop and engineering labs, you can find me analyzing New York Knicks games, maintaining a structured weightlifting and running routine, or exploring digital video production.
